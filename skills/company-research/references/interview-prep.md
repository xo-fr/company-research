# Pillar: interview_prep

What the process actually is, how long it takes, and what they ask — restricted to
reports recent enough to still be true.

**The hard rule: anything older than 18 months is actively misleading.** Companies rewrote
their loops after 2023, and again once AI-assisted candidates became normal. An outdated
question list is worse than none, because the candidate prepares for the wrong thing.

## Sources

1. **The company's own process page.** Many engineering orgs publish the loop. This is the
   most reliable source and the least used one.
2. **Hacker News**: `https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search_by_date?query=<brand>%20interview`
   — dated by construction, which is why it beats forums here.
3. **Recent blog posts and videos** by candidates, filtered hard by date.
4. **The job posting itself**, which often names the stages.
5. **Levels/Glassdoor interview sections** via your own web search, quoted as anecdote
   with the date shown on the page.

## What to produce

The loop, in order, with what each stage tests, and the observed cadence between stages.
Then question *patterns*, not question lists: "two rounds of system design focused on
data-intensive pipelines" is useful; a list of 40 leetcode titles is not.

## Signals owned by this pillar

None. This pillar informs preparation, not the verdict. Resist the urge to score it: a
hard interview is not a bad job, and an easy one is not a good one.

## What a good claim looks like

- *"Five stages: recruiter screen, take-home (4h, paid), systems interview, two
  behavioural rounds with the hiring manager, then a values round. Reported by two
  candidates in 2026-05 and 2026-07."*
- *"The take-home was replaced by a live pairing exercise in early 2026, per the
  company's own process page (updated 2026-02)."*
- Not: *"They ask standard DSA questions."* — undated, unsourced, and probably stale.

## Traps

- **Undated reports.** If you cannot date it, do not use it. Say the process could not be
  dated rather than passing on a rumour.
- **Role and level.** A new-grad loop and a staff loop share a name and nothing else.
- **Geography.** The India loop for a US company often has an extra round and a different
  bar. Match the report to the site being hired for.
- **Recruiter descriptions age badly** too — quote them with the date of the conversation.

## When to declare a gap

No dated report within 18 months: `status: "gap"`, with a fallback telling the user to ask
the recruiter directly for the stage list, which they will almost always provide.
